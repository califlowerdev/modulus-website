It never puts words in a client's mouth

This is the part most AI gets dangerously wrong. It is the first thing we fixed.

Generic tools paraphrase loosely and will invent a quote that sounds right but was never said. For a client, a single made-up line in something you ship under their name is not a typo. It is the kind of mistake that loses the account. By design, Studio works only from the source that client gave you. If a sentence is not in the recording, it cannot make it into the draft. An explicit Source Check holds every quote against the original, so nothing gets attributed to your client that they did not say.
