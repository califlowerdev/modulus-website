Before you commit

Straight answers.

Who owns the work you build?

You do. Whatever we build is built for your business, and it is yours to keep. The agents, the automations, and the setup run inside your business and serve your goals, not ours. We are not trying to lock you into a tool you can never leave. If we ever stopped working together, you would not lose what we built.

How do you handle our data?

Carefully, and with as little access as the job requires. We take only what an agent needs to do its work, nothing more, and we do not use your business data to train third-party models. We would rather scope a project tightly than hold data we do not need. For the full detail on how we treat information, see our Trust and Privacy pages.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope, and we will be honest about that on the first call rather than quote you a number before we understand the work. We do not start with a six-month project. We start by picking one real bottleneck, building the agent for that, and proving it works. A focused first piece moves faster and tells us both whether the rest is worth doing. We scope the timeline together once we have seen the actual workflow.

Do I need a technical team to use this?

No. This is built for operators, not engineers. You do not need someone on staff who can write prompts or maintain code. We build the agents to fit how you already work, roll them into your day to day, and keep them running. Your job is to run your business. Ours is to make the tools earn their place without adding to your workload.

What if automation is the wrong answer?

We tell you, before any build. Plenty of work is not worth automating, and some problems are cheaper to fix another way. If that is what we find when we map your workflows, we will say so plainly instead of selling you a project. We would rather lose a build than put an agent on work that should not have one. A clear no is worth more than a polite yes.

How do we start?

Book a short call. We will look at where the leverage actually is in your business and come back with a concrete idea, not a sales pitch. There is no commitment in the first conversation. If there is a workflow worth automating, you will leave the call knowing what it is and why.
