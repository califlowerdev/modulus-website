About Modulus

Built in the open, for the businesses the giants overlook.

I'm James Denham, and I started Modulus with one conviction: the businesses that need leverage the most are the ones who can least get it.

Big companies have engineers, automation teams, and budgets to make software do their busywork. The solo consultant, the small agency, the ministry, the family business, they do that work by hand, late at night, because no tool was ever built for them.

AI agents change that. For the first time, a small team can hand real work to something that does it end to end, not a chatbot that answers questions, but an agent that finishes the job. I'm building Modulus to put that in their hands, in the open, without the jargon or the enterprise price tag.

We are early, and I mean that as a promise, not a disclaimer. Our first product, Modulus Studio, is live today. More agents are on the way. And we judge ourselves by one thing: the work our products actually finish.

If you run a small business and you are wondering what an agent could really do for you, I would genuinely like to hear from you.
