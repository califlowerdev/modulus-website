Faithfulness, built in

Three checks run on every draft.

The part generic AI gets dangerously wrong, and the first thing we fixed. Nothing reaches you until it passes all three.

Theological Judge

Set your doctrinal frame once. Studio scores every draft against it and flags anything that drifts, so your editor can rule on it.

Source Check

Every draft is scored on how much is grounded in your source. If a line is not in the original, it does not make the draft. No invented quotes, ever.

AI Detection

Accurate is not enough if it reads like a robot. Studio scores how human each piece sounds and flags the tells, so you publish like a person.

Is there a free plan?
Yes. Creating an account gives you 25 free credits, every tool unlocked, no card needed. That covers uploading a source and making a real batch of content. Paid plans add monthly credits and full-strength source checks.
What is a credit, and what does it get me?
A credit is a unit of usage that every AI action spends. As a rough guide: a piece of content runs about 4 credits and an hour of audio transcription about 18. So Starter's 600 credits a month is roughly 150 pieces of content, or about 30 hours of transcription. The app shows an estimate before each action and your balance as you work, so nothing is a surprise.
What happens if I run out?
Add a top-up pack anytime, or your credits refill next month.
