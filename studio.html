Faithfulness, built in

Three checks run on every draft.

This is the part generic AI gets dangerously wrong, and it is the first thing we fixed. Nothing reaches you until it has passed all three.

Theological Judge

Set your doctrinal frame once, a confession, a statement of faith, or your house's editorial standard, and Studio scores every draft against it and flags anything that drifts. It does not decide what is true. It holds the line you drew, so an editor can rule on it.

Source Check

Every draft is scored on how much of it is grounded in your source versus invented. If a sentence is not in the book, the article, or the talk, it does not make it into the draft. No made-up quotes, no statistics pulled from thin air, ever attributed to your author.

AI Detection

A draft can be accurate and still read like a robot wrote it. Studio scores how human each piece sounds and surfaces the tells, em-dashes, stock AI phrasing, the patterns that quietly cost you credibility, so what you publish reads like a person, not a prompt.
